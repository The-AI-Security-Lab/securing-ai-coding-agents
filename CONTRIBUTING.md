# Contributing to Securing AI Coding Agents

Thank you for helping expand AI Security Lab's practical security education and verification workflows.

## What we welcome

- Synthetic coding-agent security scenarios and evaluation cases.
- Reproducible Claude Code and Codex configuration examples.
- Deterministic verification checks, harness improvements, and evidence formats.
- Clearer participant instructions, accessibility improvements, and troubleshooting.
- New agent support only when tested and documented.

## Before submitting

Open an issue to discuss substantial changes. Keep pull requests focused and explain the security property being tested, expected outcomes, how to reproduce results, and known limitations. Add tests or evidence where feasible. Clearly distinguish tested behavior from vendor-documented or proposed behavior.

## Safety and provenance

Use synthetic data only. Do not submit credentials, personal information, customer or employer code, production configurations, real incident data, or material you lack permission to contribute. Do not include exploit steps aimed at third-party systems. Disclose third-party code, data, and license requirements; do not copy proprietary material into the repository.

## Licensing and contributor rights

Review `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CODE.md`, and `NOTICE.md` before contributing. Keep original attribution and identify your modifications. You retain copyright in your original contributions unless separately agreed. A pull request does **not** automatically assign your copyright to AI Security Lab or grant blanket commercial relicensing rights. The maintainers may request a separate contributor agreement before merging substantial contributions if future commercial licensing requires it. Do not represent a contribution as accepted or commercially licensed until maintainers confirm.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it and agree that, if accepted, it may be published under the applicable repository license, subject to any separately agreed contributor terms. If you cannot grant those rights, explain the limitation before submitting.

## Review expectations

Maintainers may ask for synthetic fixtures, reproducible tests, license clarification, or narrower scope. Please do not assume that a proposed control is enforced merely because a configuration file contains it; provide runtime and independent outcome evidence when claiming verification.

## Conduct and commercial requests

Be respectful and constructive. For permission to use workshop materials in paid training or consulting, or distribute the harness in a commercial product, contact AI Security Lab through https://www.aisecuritylab.com/ .
